01 · INTRODUCTION

Daily work, not a vague to-do list

T2T2 organizes daily study, revision, mock tests and progress reporting around the student’s selected CA level and attempt date. Instead of relying on memory, the student gets a structured plan.

🎯

Core idea

Convert a large syllabus into small daily actions.

📚

Three levels

CA Foundation, Intermediate and Final routes.

🤝

Documented scope

Study tracking, weekly review, analytics, chapter tracking and faculty reports. No built-in student chat or forum.

02 · FEATURES

What the planner actually does

⚡

Daily Execution

A ranked list of today’s study, revision and practice tasks, so the next action is clear.

Mark a task complete and recall sessions get scheduled automatically. Unfinished work stays visible as backlog to be re-planned.

Example: finish a core Accounting task today, then see its recall sessions appear on later dates, alongside Law revision and a Quant practice set.
🗓️

Weekly Review & mock tests

The seven-day plan in one view, with a completion indicator per day. It exposes uneven patterns, like a huge Monday and a near-empty Tuesday.

Schedule mocks and record results. Submitted results are locked, so they can’t be quietly edited later.

📊

Analytics

Study hours vs target, backlog trends, paper-wise risk and revision health, turned into a progress overview.

Example: the paper flagged highest-risk becomes the priority for revision or extra practice.
📖

Chapter tracking

Progress at chapter level, not a rough subject-level guess. Mark complete, rate confidence, and see how many recall sessions each chapter received.

Weak chapters stay flagged until a later revision or mock addresses them.

📨

Accountability reports

A weekly report with the student’s name, study hours, backlog, 1-4-7-21 adherence and mock scores, shareable via WhatsApp or email.

Gives faculty a clearer view than an anonymous summary.

🧭

Levels & access

Open your CA level and set an attempt date to organize prep around your own timeline.

A faculty Class Register exists (use it on the office machine). Some preview buttons are demo-only, so don’t confuse them with live features.

04 · ONLINE CLASSES & COMMUNICATION

A limited but useful bridge

Online classes can make it harder to ask questions, compare understanding or get peer support, especially when students hesitate to speak up. That’s a general online-learning concern, not a planner result.

✅ What T2T2 offers

  • A named weekly report to share with faculty
  • Hours, backlog, revision adherence and mock scores as a starting point for a progress talk

⚠️ What it doesn’t document

  • Direct messaging
  • Class discussion or Q&A threads
  • Peer study groups
  • Live interaction

So it shouldn’t be pitched as a full solution to the student-to-student communication gap. Its documented role is individual planning and faculty-facing accountability.

05 · BENEFITS, LIMITATIONS, CONCLUSION

Strengths and honest limits

💪 Strengths

  • Connects daily execution, spaced revision, weekly planning, mock records, chapter confidence, analytics and faculty reporting
  • Shows what was planned, done, pending and at risk

🧱 Limitations

  • Depends on accurate input and consistent use
  • Analytics are only as good as the data recorded
  • No built-in peer communication
T2T2 is a study organization and accountability planner, not a complete online learning or peer-communication platform.

It helps CA students structure preparation and makes progress easier to discuss with faculty. Peer interaction and doubt-solving need other channels or future features.
